Traditional Chinese medicine (TCM) is a branch of traditional medicine from China. It is at least 3,500 years old. Treatments include herbal medicine, acupuncture, cupping, massage, and exercise. TCM's methods are different from Western medicine, so it is hard to find evidence that TCM is safe and helpful. Many of TCM's most important ideas are based on the teachings of Daoism.
