The Metropolitan Museum of Art is an art museum. It is sometimes called The Met. It is found on the eastern edge of Central Park in New York City. It has a collection of more than two million works of art. The main building is one of the world's largest art galleries, at about 2,000,000 square feet (190,000 m2) in area.
