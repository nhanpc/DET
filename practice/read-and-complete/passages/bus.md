A bus is a large wheeled vehicle meant to carry many passengers along with the bus driver. It is larger than a car or van, but smaller than the average rail transport. They are most often used for public transport, but they are also in use for charters, and some are privately owned. Although the average bus carries between 30 and 100 passengers, some buses can carry up to 300 passengers.
