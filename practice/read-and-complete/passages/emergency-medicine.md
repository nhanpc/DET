Emergency medicine is a part of medicine that focuses on caring for people whose life or health is in immediate danger, for example from a serious injury, heart attack, or poisoning. Emergency Medicine (abbreviation EM) is sometimes also called Accident and Emergency Medicine (AEM). EM doctors specialize in treating diseases and injuries that need immediate care. These kind of diseases or injuries are called emergencies. If they are not helped quickly, the person may become more sick or even die.
