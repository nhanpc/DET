The Mediterranean diet is a type of diet (a way of eating). It is a type of plant-based diet (like vegetarianism). The Mediterranean diet is not only food. It is also the skills and traditions people use to make it, for example farming, fishing, and cooking. It is also the traditions and culture about food and meals.
