Open-wheel car describes cars with the wheels outside the car's main body and often with one seat. Other names include formula car, single-seater car in British English. Open-wheel cars are different from street cars, sports cars, stock cars, and touring cars. These car have their wheels below or inside the body or fenders. Open-wheel cars are usually built just for racing, including formula racing.
