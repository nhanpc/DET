A gate is a place between two parts of a wall, or a fence which could be opened and closed to let people go through it. In the Middle Ages, a city gate also allowed people to go into a (walled) city. Nowadays, people also talk about gates at airports. When going on an aircraft, people need to get together in a certain area of the airport first.
