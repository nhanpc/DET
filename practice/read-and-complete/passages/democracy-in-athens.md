The Democracy in Ancient Athens was very different from modern democracy. Citizens had the right to help govern but most people were not citizens. To be classed as a citizen in fifth-century Athens you had to be male, born from two Athenian parents, over eighteen years old, and completed your military service. Women, slaves, metics and children under the age of 20 were not allowed to become citizens.
