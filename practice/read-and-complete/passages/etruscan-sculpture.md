Etruscan sculpture was an important form of art made by the Etruscans. They lived in parts of northern and central Italy from the 9th century BC to the 1st century BC. Their art was influenced by Greek art, but it also had its own style. Because very few written texts from the Etruscans survive, much of what we know about them comes from their art. Their sculpture helped historians understand their culture and history. It also influenced early Roman art.
