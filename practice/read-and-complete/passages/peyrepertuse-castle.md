Peyrepertuse Castle is a castle in France. It is one of the Cathar castles. It was built before the 11th century. It is in Aude, in Occitanie. It is built in a strategic position, on a cliff, about 30-40m high. The castle is first mentioned in the year 806, but the site seems to have been occupied since Roman times. At that time, it belonged to a Catalan count.
