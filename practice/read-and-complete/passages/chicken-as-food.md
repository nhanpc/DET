Chicken is the most common type of poultry around the world. It is prepared in a number of ways, including boiling, baking, frying, grilling. Prepared chicken became a staple food for fast food production in the second half of the 20th century CE. Chicken is more healthy than red meat. It has less cholesterol and saturated fat. Chicken meat was first eaten in Babylonia in 600 BCE.
