A ship is a large vehicle used to travel on water. It is bigger than a boat. Most are cargo ships, which carry most of the world's international trade. There are also many warships, passenger ships and other kinds for different purposes. When people talk about a ship, they often use the pronoun "she". For example: She was launched on 29 December 1878.
