People have lived in Australia for over 65,000 years. The first people who arrived in Australia were the Aboriginals and the Torres Strait Islanders. They lived in all parts of Australia. They lived by hunting, fishing and gathering. Aboriginals and Torres Strait Islanders invented tools like the boomerang, spear, and there is evidence that they used farming methods. Tradition was very important in their lives.
