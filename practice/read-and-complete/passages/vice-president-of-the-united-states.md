The vice president of the United States (VPOTUS) is the second highest executive officer of the U.S. federal government after the president of the United States. The vice president ranks first in the presidential line of succession and is also the officer of the legislative branch, president of the Senate and the presiding officer of the Senate. JD Vance is the 50th and current vice president of the United States, in office since January 20, 2025.
