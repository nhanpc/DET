Infection is the invasion of an organism's body tissues by things that cause disease. The disease is caused by the invading agents multiplying. As they multiply, they can form toxins and damage the organism's tissues. Infectious disease, also known as 'transmissible disease' or 'communicable disease', is illness resulting from an infection.
