A bicycle path is a way marked for people on bicycles to use. Bike paths became increasingly popular as more people took on the recreational sport of cycling. Some bike paths are unpaved for mountain bikes and gravel bikes, and others are paved. Many bicycle path networks can be found in countries such as Denmark or Netherlands, which has had a national system of cycle routes since 1993.
