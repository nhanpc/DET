The Oort cloud, Oort's cloud, or Öpik–Oort cloud, is a cloud of comets and other objects. Astronomers believe it is way beyond the orbits of Pluto and the Kuiper belt. The Oort cloud is believed to be the source of long-period comets in the Solar System. The Oort cloud may lie about 50,000 AU, or nearly a light-year, from the Sun.
