The skin is the outer covering of mammals and some other animals. It has many important jobs. First, it helps protect the body by stopping pathogens such as bacteria and parasites from getting inside. Second, it helps control the body's temperature. It does this by allowing evaporation from the skin and by making changes to help the body stay at the right temperature. The skin is a physical barrier.
