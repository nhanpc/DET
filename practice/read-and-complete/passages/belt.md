A belt is a piece of clothing worn around a person's waist. Belts can be used to hold up trousers (pants, men) or skirts (women) or for carrying things in pockets, such as on a utility belt, which police use. Belts are commonly made using cloth or leather. They are worn by men and women, however men wear them more casually and formally. The most common colors are black and brown. Belts have buckles.
