Sleep (also called napping) is a state of resting. It happens in most animals, including humans. To sleep is to be unconscious. During deep sleep, most of the muscles that animals can control are not active, but resting to regain the energy they need for the next time they wake up. When asleep, humans cannot tell what is happening around them. Like most animals, healthy sleep in humans most often happens at night.
