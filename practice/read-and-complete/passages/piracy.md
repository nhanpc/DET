Piracy can mean different things; it can be a crime that is committed. A human on a ship at sea who steals from other ships is called a pirate. Pirates usually have small, fast boats. Pirates use such boats to attack other ships, which are usually large cargo ships. For as long as ships have sailed the sea, there have been pirates. Ancient Egypt and the Romans, Medieval kings, and the British Empire dealt with pirates.
