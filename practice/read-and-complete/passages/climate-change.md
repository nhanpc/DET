Climate change refers to long-term changes in temperature, precipitation, and other atmospheric conditions on Earth. When people talk about climate change, they are usually talking about the problem of human-caused global warming, which is happening now. But the climate of the Earth has changed over not just thousands of years, but tens or hundreds of millions of years. It has alternated between hotter and colder periods, with some extremes like ice ages.
