Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only place known where life exists. One big reason life can exist here is that Earth has a lot of water on its surface, being the only planet in our Solar System with liquid water. Most of Earth’s water is in the oceans, which cover about 71% of the planet’s surface. The rest, about 29%, is land.
