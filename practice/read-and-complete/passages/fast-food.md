Fast food is the term for a kind of food that people eat from a restaurant, cafe or take-out where food is prepared and served quickly. It is mass-produced food. It is often western pattern and pre-prepared and delivered to the shop ready for frying or boiling. There is a strong priority on "speed of service". The restaurants that sell fast food are called "fast food shops" or "fast-food restaurants".
