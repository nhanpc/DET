The Internet is the world’s largest global communication network for computers and other devices. It connects many smaller networks from homes, schools, businesses, and governments. These networks share different kinds of information. The short form of the Internet is “the Net.” One of its most popular services is the World Wide Web, which is used by billions of people every day. The Internet was developed in the United States by the Department of Defence Advanced Research Projects Agency (DARPA).
