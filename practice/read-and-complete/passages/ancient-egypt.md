Ancient Egypt was a society that began around 3150 BC and lasted until 30 BC, when it was invaded by the Romans. Egypt grew along the Nile River and was at its most powerful in the 2nd millennium BC. Its land stretched from the Nile Delta to Nubia, a kingdom located mostly in present-day Sudan. With the water from the Nile, crops were generally good. They were grown after the water from the Nile floods went down.
