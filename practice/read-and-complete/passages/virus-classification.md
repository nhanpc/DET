Virus classification is the way viruses are put into groups by scientists. There are many different kinds of viruses. Scientists classify viruses to make it easier to learn about them. Classification also helps scientists to remember viruses and the diseases they cause. The International Committee of Taxonomy of Viruses (ICTV) is in charge of virus classification. Sometimes different viruses have things in common. This is used in classification.
