A coat is a piece of clothing that is worn over a person's upper body. It can be used to keep warm or dry (a raincoat). A coat may also be worn by someone because of how it looks. A coat usually has long sleeves, and is fastened at the front. Some coats have collars, shoulder straps or hoods. One type of coat is a jacket.
