The Titan stick insect is one of the largest stick insects in Australia. The scientific name is Acrophylla titan. It has a brown body with spikes on its neck. The Titan stick insect can reach up to 27 centimetres (11 in) in length. It takes the form of a stick as a disguise to predators. The Titan stick insect can be found in two states of Australia; Queensland and New South Wales.
