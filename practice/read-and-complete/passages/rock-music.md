Rock music is a genre of popular music. It developed in the late 1900s, chiefly in the United States. It originally started in the 1940s and 1950s with the start of rock and roll. "Rock and roll" grew out of R&B (Rhythm & Blues) and country after the electrification of the guitar (and music in general) in the 1940s. Rock has a lot in common with other genres like blues and folk.
