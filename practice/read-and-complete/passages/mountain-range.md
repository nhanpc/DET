A mountain range, also called mountain chain or mountain belt, is a geographic area with many mountains. A mountain system, or system of mountain ranges, includes geological features that are in the same region as a mountain range. Mountain ranges usually include highlands or mountain passes and valleys. Individual mountains in the same range do not always have the same geology or petrology.
