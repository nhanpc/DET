Elk Grove Village is a village in northeastern Illinois near the O'Hare International Airport and is a near northwest suburb, touching the city of Chicago. It is one of the Chicago metropolitan area's main villages because of the large industrial park on the eastern border of the village. The village is mostly in Cook County with a small part in DuPage County. 32,812 people lived there at the 2020 census.
