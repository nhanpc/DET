Terrace farming (also called step farming) is a method of farming that consists of different "steps" or terraces that were developed in various places around the world. The rice terraces of the Philippine Cordilleras are an example and are considered a World Heritage Site. This method of farming uses "steps" that are built into the side of a mountain or hill. On each level, various crops are planted.
