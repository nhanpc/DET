A recycling bin (or recycle bin) is a container that holds recyclables, such as glass, metal, paper or plastic before they are taken to recycling centers. Recycling bins usually have the recycling symbol to make them look different from normal trash cans. The color blue or green is also used often as well. In computers, the Recycle Bin is a built-in application that comes with the Microsoft Windows operating system.
