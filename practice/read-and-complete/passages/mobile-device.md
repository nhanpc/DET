A mobile device is an electronic device which its user can easily carry with them. Typical examples are a mobile phone and handheld game consoles or tablets. Mobile devices do not need an external power supply to work. Usually, they have a battery to hold electricity to work without electric wires. A user can change, send and move data by connecting to a modem or cable, or more often wirelessly.
