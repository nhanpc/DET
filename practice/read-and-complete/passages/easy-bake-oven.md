The Easy-Bake Oven was a popular children's toy stove for baking made by Hasbro. It used electricity for heat and came with pans and other equipment. Different models were made in many colors. The toy was first sold in 1963. Kids would be able to bake small cakes and cookies as long as they had electricity. The oven was supposed to be used with adult supervision.
