An island is a piece of land that is surrounded by a body of water such as a lake, river, sea or ocean. Islands are smaller than continents. Although there are many Islands that are surrounded by fresh water, the vast majority of them are surrounded by oceans. Greenland and Australia are huge islands, but they are built of continental rock, and the latter is generally considered a continent.
