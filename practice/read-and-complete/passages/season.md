A season is a part of a year. Many areas of the Earth have four seasons in a year: spring, summer, autumn and winter. Some areas have a different number of seasons. In places which are tropical and subtropical, there are two seasons: the rainy (or wet, or monsoon) season and the dry season. This is because the rain changes more than the temperature.
