Law is a set of rules made by a particular place or authority to keep society safe and peaceful. Courts or police help to make sure these rules are followed. They can punish people who break the laws, for example by making them pay a fine or by sending them to jail. In ancient societies, leaders wrote laws that told people how they should live, work and do business with each other.
