Vitamin D is a hormone. It is a steroid that is made in the body under the right conditions. It is stored in the fat (lipid) in the body. The body needs sunlight, which acts on the lower layers of the skin to make vitamin D. If the body does not make enough then it can be found from food sources in tiny amounts.
