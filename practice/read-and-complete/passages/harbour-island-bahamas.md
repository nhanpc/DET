Harbour Island is an island and district in the Bahamas. It is off the northeast coast of Eleuthera Island. The only town on the island is Dunmore Town. Harbour Island is famous for its pink sand beaches. These are found all along the east side of the island. Harbour Island is part of the Out Islands of the Bahamas.
