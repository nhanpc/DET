Oxygen is a chemical element. It has the symbol O and atomic number 8. It is a colorless, odorless, and tasteless gas at 25 degrees Celsius at Standard atmosphere (a pressure). Oxygen is part of the air we breathe. It makes up about 21% of Earth’s atmosphere. It is the third most common element in the universe, after hydrogen and helium. Oxygen is part of the chalcogen group on the periodic table.
