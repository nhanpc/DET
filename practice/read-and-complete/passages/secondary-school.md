Secondary school is the next step up from primary school. Secondary schools are often called high schools or middle schools in the United States. In the United Kingdom, secondary schools may be public schools, grammar schools, state schools or comprehensive schools. In Australia, a person will generally begin secondary school when they are at the age of 12 and finish (or graduate) when they are either 16 or 18 years of age.
