A psychiatric hospital is a residential facility where people with mental illnesses can be kept safe and receive treatment. On some occasions, a person might choose to enter a psychiatric hospital to address their mental health. On other occasions, people can be forced into psychiatric facilities if a doctor thinks they are a danger to others or themselves. Doctors who work at psychiatric hospitals are called psychiatrists.
