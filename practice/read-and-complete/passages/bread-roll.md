A bread roll is a piece of bread, usually small and round and is usually a side dish. Bread rolls are most times used in the same way as sandwiches are - cut in half, with items like butter placed between the two halves. There are many names for bread rolls, especially in local versions of British English. These were named depending on how the dough was made and how the roll was cooked.
