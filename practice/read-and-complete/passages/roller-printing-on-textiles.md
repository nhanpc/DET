Roller printing is a textile printing process created by Thomas Bell of Scotland in 1783. He was trying to make copperplate printing cost less. This method was used in Lancashire cloth mills to produce cotton dress cloth from the 1790s. Advancements in the technology resulted in more elaborate roller prints in bright, rich colours from the 1820s.
