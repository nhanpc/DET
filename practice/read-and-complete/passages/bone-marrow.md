Bone marrow is a type of tissue that can be found in hollow bones of many animals. In humans, it is soft, spongy tissue where blood stem cells live. Blood stem cells produce all your essential blood cells, such as red blood cells to carry oxygen and white blood cells to fight infection like a factory produces things. Bone marrow makes up about 4% of an adult human's weight (about 2.6 kilograms).
