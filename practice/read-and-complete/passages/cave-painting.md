Cave paintings are paintings on cave walls and ceilings. Usually these paintings were made in prehistoric times. It is not known why these paintings were made. They may have been used in rituals. Some may have provided information. Many paintings in caves are difficult to get to. Today, there are about 350 caves known to have paintings in them. Many are in France and Spain.
