The name diesel is given to an engine invented by a German named Rudolf Diesel in the late 19th century. It is one of the most used kinds of internal combustion engine. Most other internal combustion engines need an ignition system that uses an electric spark to burn the mix of fuel and air and make power. Some other types of ignition systems use compressed air from an outside source, such as an air compressor. A diesel does not.
