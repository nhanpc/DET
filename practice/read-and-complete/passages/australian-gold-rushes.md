The Australian gold rush was a large number of gold discoveries in Australia. Thousands of people came to Australia in the hope of finding gold and becoming rich. The rush started in 1851 when gold was found near Bathurst, New South Wales and ended with the last rush in 1893 to Kalgoorlie, Western Australia. At each place gold was easily found in rivers and creeks.
