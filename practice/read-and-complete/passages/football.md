"Football" is a word which could mean one of several sports. The best-known type of football is association football. This is called "soccer" in the United States, Canada, Australia and Ireland to avoid confusion with American football, Canadian football, Australian rules football and Gaelic football. The name "football" comes from the two words "foot" and "ball".
