A fire alarm system is an electronic computer that looks for a fire in a building. It can see or detect the fire by using smoke detectors, heat detectors, manual pull stations, or other kinds of electronics. When a fire happens, the fire alarm system makes a loud, high-pitched sound to notify people that there is a fire in the building. The sounders can be programmed to make different tones for different kinds of emergencies.
