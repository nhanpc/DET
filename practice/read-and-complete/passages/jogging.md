Jogging or going for a jog is running at a slow speed. Many people do it to keep fit and healthy. People go jogging for exercise, not for competitions. Jogging is a very cheap way of keeping fit, because all that is needed is a good pair of running shoes. One can jog anywhere, although it is good to stay away from main roads where the air is full of traffic fumes.
