Great Wall Station is a science station in Antarctica from the country of China. It is the first Chinese station in Antarctica and is open all year round. The station works with other nearby stations from different countries. They share things and help each other with supplies. These stations are from Chile, Russia, Uruguay, Korea, and Brazil.
