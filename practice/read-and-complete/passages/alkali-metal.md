Alkali metals are the chemical elements in periodic table group 1, other than hydrogen. The term group 1 element can be used to include hydrogen. They are silver-coloured when pure, soft and have only one valence electron. They like to make reactions in which they give up this single electron and then have a charge of +1. They react strongly with water and explode when wet, and because of this, they have to be stored in oil.
