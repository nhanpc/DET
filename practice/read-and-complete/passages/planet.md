A planet is a large object such as Venus or Earth that orbits a star. Planets do not make light but can reflect light. Planets are spherical (that is, ball-shaped), because their gravity compresses them into a sphere. Jupiter is the biggest planet in the Solar System, while the smallest planet in the Solar System is Mercury. Planets are shaped like a slightly squashed ball (called a spheroid). Objects that orbit planets are called satellites.
