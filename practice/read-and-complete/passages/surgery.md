Surgery is an operation that involves cutting, taking out, changing or fixing parts of the human body. Doctors who do surgeries are called surgeons. Many surgeons are experts in certain areas; for example brain or heart or eye surgery. One who operates on bones is an orthopaedic surgeon. Surgery (operations) can be simple, like taking a tooth out. Surgery can also be complicated, like repairing a damaged heart. Operating rooms have to be very clean places.
