Mount Magnet is a town in the Mid West region of Western Australia. It is one of the oldest gold mining towns in the region and has been a place for gold mining for a very long time. The town is named after a nearby hill, which explorer Robert Austin named in 1854 because its rocks affected his compass. The Aboriginal name for this hill is Warramboo, which means "campfire camping place.".
