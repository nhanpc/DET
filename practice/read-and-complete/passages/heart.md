The heart is a hard-working, fist-sized muscle found in people and animals. It pumps out blood through tube-like structures called blood vessels. The heart and these vessels together make up the body-wide circulatory system. Blood carries in oxygen-rich air and nutrient-packed food to the body and takes away waste-filled substances like carbon dioxide to the lungs.
