# Interactive Reading, Interactive Listening and the Samples

No drill here yet — notes on how to practise these outside the app until one
exists. The timings are in [docs/det-format.md](../../docs/det-format.md).

## Interactive Reading (7–8 min per set, 2 sets)

A passage in several steps: complete the sentences (a drop-down per gap),
complete the passage (pick the missing sentence), highlight the answer to a
question, pick the idea the passage supports, and the title. Practice:

- Read one Simple English Wikipedia or news paragraph a day against the clock
  (3 min), then answer: main idea in one line, one sentence that supports it,
  one word you would replace. The same passages can go into
  `../read-and-complete/passages/` afterwards.
- The gap steps are the cloze drill in disguise: `#drill/read-and-complete`
  in passage mode is the closest thing in the app.

## Interactive Listening (6 min 30 s + 75 s summary, 2 sets)

Since July 2025 each set starts with *Listen and Complete* (3–4 blanks in an
audio scenario), then a conversation where you pick your side's line, then a
75-second written summary. Practice:

- Dictation (`#drill/listen-and-type`) for the blanks and for holding a
  sentence in memory.
- After any podcast or lecture clip (2–3 min), write a 75-second summary in
  `#drill/read-then-write` mode with the clock set mentally to 75 s — or type
  it here in a dated note and rate it with the same four lines.

## Interactive Speaking (35 s per answer, 6–8 questions per set)

A simulated conversation; the next question depends on your answer. The
nearest drill is `#drill/listen-then-speak` (a spoken prompt, then record):
aim to answer in the first 5 seconds and fill 30 seconds with two reasons and
an example.

## Writing Sample (5 min) and Speaking Sample (3 min)

Ungraded but sent with the score. `#drill/read-then-write` and
`#drill/read-then-speak` with the long timers are the same shape; keep the
drafts in `../writing/drafts/` like every other attempt.
